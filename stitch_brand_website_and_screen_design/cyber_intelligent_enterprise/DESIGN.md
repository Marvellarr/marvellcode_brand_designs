## Brand & Style
This design system defines a high-performance, futuristic cyber-enterprise aesthetic tailored for next-generation engineering platforms, data ecosystems, and digital product agencies. It embodies the ethos of "Ideas / Systems / Impact" by pairing crystalline structure with luminous kinetic energy.

The aesthetic bridges technical rigor and high-impact digital craftsmanship:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep space dark mode dominated by obsidian and midnight navy (`#0B1020`, `#0F172A`), providing an infinite backdrop for luminous signals.
- **Energy:** High-octane transitions driven by electric blue (`#2563EB`) and radiant violet (`#8B5CF6`), used as directional laser lines, progressive gradient strokes, and ambient backdrops.
- **Glass & Structure:** Multi-layered translucent acrylic panels with 1px border glows, backdrop filters, and crisp geometry that emphasize precision without visual clutter.
- **Tone & Voice:** Authoritative, razor-sharp, sophisticated, and pioneering.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high dynamic range contrast against nocturnal substrates, utilizing laser blues and electromagnetic violets to signal intelligence and interactivity.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#2563EB`):** High-velocity electric blue used for key calls-to-action, active component states, and primary focal highlights.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6`):** Luminous purple-violet delivering creative depth, futuristic status indicators, and gradient pairings.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`):** Celestial cyan used for micro-interactions, metrics, and terminal code glyph accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B1020`):** Deep space obsidian providing true dark-canvas foundation, complemented by `#0F172A` (slate base) and `#1E293B` (elevated surface container).

### Color Rules & Gradients
- **Hyper-Gradient:** Linear `135deg` transition from `#2563EB` to `#8B5CF6`. Reserved for hero badges, progress tracks, active interactive rings, and brand focal marks.
- **Text Hierarchies:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary titles and metrics; high-clarity icy gray (`#F1F5F9`) for secondary headlines; cool muted slate (`#94A3B8`) for body prose and descriptive labels; deep muted (`#475569`) for subtle metadata and deactivated states.
- **Glow & Border Strategy:** Surfaces leverage low-opacity border overlays (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)` or `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`) to yield crisp, illuminated edges against dark backdrops.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the bold architectural geometry of Montserrat with the neutral readability of Inter.

- **Montserrat (Headings & Impact Display):** Characterized by wide geometric construction, high capitals, and crisp angled terminals. Used in full uppercase tracking for high-level enterprise badges (`IDEAS / SYSTEMS / IMPACT`) and bold, assertive weights (`700`, `800`) for landing headers, KPI metrics, and card titles.
- **Inter (Body, Tables & UI Controls):** Optimized for continuous reading on dark backdrops. Medium and regular weights ensure legibility across dense dashboard modules, telemetry data, and form states.
- **Monospace Pairing (JetBrains Mono):** Introduced specifically for system identifiers, API parameters, and syntax elements, reinforcing the engineering authority of the platform.

## Layout & Spacing
Layouts follow a fluid, responsive 12-column grid system tuned for high-density enterprise monitors as well as touch-responsive mobile views.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column dynamic framework with a maximum container boundary of `1440px`, centered with `margin` margins (`3rem`) and `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet views compress to an 8-column layout (`1.5rem` margins), while mobile breaks down to a 4-column stack with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Rhythms step along a base 4px/8px incremental grid. Standard intra-component paddings utilize `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px), while component separation and stack cards leverage `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px).
- **Dashboard Docking:** Complex tools deploy a permanent collapsed/expanded utility rail on the left (64px to 260px) paired with scroll-independent workspace canvas panes.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through luminous glassmorphic layering and edge illumination rather than conventional drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** Void dark `#0B1020` textured with a soft, deep gradient radial glow centered behind key hero zones (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)` blur 120px).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Shells):** Translucent obsidian `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` with `16px` backdrop-blur filter and an ultra-subtle border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States):** Surface brightness increases to `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with a combined drop aura: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`, paired with an illuminated border switch: `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Command Palettes & Flyouts):** Solidified obsidian-slate tint `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95)` with dual shadow ring: outer ambient blur `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` and inner specular glow `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.

## Shapes
The design adopts a refined, medium-rounded geometry (level `2`) that balances geometric cyber precision with modern tactile comfort.

- **Standard Inputs, Badges & Buttons:** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px) ensures buttons, chips, and field controls retain architectural structure without harsh angular points.
- **Card Shells & Dashboards:** Containers and glass panes use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to soften larger surface silhouettes.
- **Flyout Modals & Floating Banners:** Elevated panels leverage `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) providing polished app-grade framing.
- **Terminal & Metric Pills:** Micro status chips and metric counters adopt fully pill-rounded formats (`9999px`) to immediately contrast against rigid data grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid electric-to-violet gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB 0%, #8B5CF6 100%)`), text in pure white (`Montserrat`, 600 weight). Hover activates a luminous glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.5)`) and slight brightness boost.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Clear transparent glass with `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`. Text in `#F1F5F9`. Hover fills container to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` with crisp white text.
- **Icon / Utility Action:** Square 40x40px container, `rounded-md`, filled with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)` and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact uppercase labels (`Inter`, label-sm, tracking `0.1em`). Green-cyan for live/stable (`#10B981`), violet for processing (`#8B5CF6`), and electric blue for standard context. Backgrounds are low-opacity tint (12%) with matching colored border (25%).
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Soft background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.5)` transitioning to solid electric blue accent on active selection.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Dark tinted fields (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Height: 44px, padding: `0 16px`. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- **Focus Treatment:** Smooth transition to `border-color: #2563EB` paired with an electric neon aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px squared with 4px border radius. Inactive: dark slate border (`#475569`). Checked: electric blue background containing a crisp white vector checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric dual ring, outer border glowing in `#8B5CF6` on selection with an inner centered `#2563EB` dot.

### Cards & Modules
- **Metric / KPI Cards:** Framed in `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` over translucent dark slate. Header features monospace parameter IDs followed by large display numerals in Montserrat (`700`), flanked by directional mini sparklines in electric cyan.
- **Feature / Service Cards:** Incorporates glowing accent top-borders created with gradient masks (`#2563EB` to `#8B5CF6`), revealing an ambient hover sweep across the card body on cursor hover.

### Code Terminals & Telemetry Logs
- **Embedded Console Blocks:** Deep `#070A13` background with an upper control toolbar displaying traffic light indicator dots (`#EF4444`, `#F59E0B`, `#10B981`), JetBrains Mono text formatting, and subtle syntax lighting in electric blue and luminous violet.
